Welcome to Te Hepara Pai

Te Hepara Pai is part of Te Pihopatanga o Aotearoa | The Maori Anglican Church in New Zealand. We are in Te Hui Amorangi ki Te Upoko o te Ika. Here is everything you need for your first visit.

Where

Te Hepara Pai Church is located at 131 Cole Street Masterton, near Douglas Park School.

  • The church can be accessed from Essex Street via the walkway through Douglas Park.
  • There is parking on the street outside and there is an Accessibility Park inside the grounds.
  • The Church and Marae are fully accessible.
  • We are within walking distance of St Matthew’s Collegiate, Wairarapa College and approximately a 20‑minute walk from the town centre.

How

We are a multi-cultural congregation and our services actively involve the congregation. All readings, including the Old Testament, New Testament, and the Psalms, are delivered on a rotational basis by our regular church attendees. Similarly, our Prayers of the People are offered by members of the congregation ensuring we capture the thanks and concerns of the wider community.

  • Morning tea is shared after our Sunday services and we encourage you to bring a small plate of food to share.
  • We support our wider community by maintaining a food donation basket for the Masterton Foodbank. We welcome small contributions of non-perishable food items for local people in need.
  • To help maintain church operations, we kindly request a small, optional koha during services. Every dollar assists in keeping our church running.

When

Our regular Sunday morning service begins at 9:30 am and typically lasts about an hour, followed by morning tea.

We also hold additional services on special occasions such as Christmas, Ash Wednesday, Good Friday and Easter Sunday.

Instead of Sunday School, we offer Atrium, a fun after-school activity, on Thursdays from 3:30 pm to 4:45 pm.

Our purpose

We seek Te Oranga Ake o Te Iwi, o te Ao, the expression of God’s Kingdom through the flourishing of all people and of all creation.

People of all ages, cultures, genders and faiths are welcome at Te Hepara Pai. Our communion services follow the New Zealand Prayer Book and we sing a range of hymns in Maori and English.

Coming for the first time?

Answers to the questions people ask before their first Sunday.

What time is the Sunday service, and how long does it go?

Our regular Sunday morning service begins at 9:30 am and typically lasts about an hour, followed by morning tea. We also hold additional services on special occasions such as Christmas, Ash Wednesday, Good Friday and Easter Sunday.

Where do I park, and is the church accessible?

There is parking on the street outside 131 Cole Street and an Accessibility Park inside the grounds. You can also walk in from Essex Street through Douglas Park. The Church and Marae are fully accessible.

Do I need to bring anything?

Nothing is required. Morning tea is shared after our Sunday services and we encourage you to bring a small plate of food to share. A small, optional koha is welcome during services, and we keep a food donation basket for the Masterton Foodbank if you would like to bring non-perishable items.

What is the service like?

People of all ages, cultures, genders and faiths are welcome. Our communion services follow the New Zealand Prayer Book and we sing a range of hymns in Maori and English. Readings and the Prayers of the People are shared by members of the congregation on a rotation.

Is there anything for children?

Instead of Sunday School, we offer Atrium, a fun after-school activity, on Thursdays from 3:30 pm to 4:45 pm during the school term. See the Atrium page for how a session runs.

Can we book Te Rangamarie Marae?

Yes. The marae hosts hui, wānanga, retreats and community gatherings. To enquire about dates, costs and bookings, contact Bishop Wai Quayle on 027 240 9261 or by email (see the Te Rangamarie Marae page).
