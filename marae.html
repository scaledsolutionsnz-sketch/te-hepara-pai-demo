Te Rangamarie Marae

A warm, welcoming space for hui, wānanga, retreats and community gatherings, right beside the church.

Book our facilities

Te Rangamarie Marae is a warm, welcoming space for hui, wānanga, retreats and community gatherings. Our wharenui, wharekai and shared spaces are well cared for, with plenty of room for sleeping, meeting and sharing kai together.

We’re also working to upgrade our facilities, with new, modern bathroom areas opening in 2027 to make your stay even more comfortable.

  • Wharenui, wharekai and shared spaces
  • The Church and Marae are fully accessible, with an Accessibility Park inside the grounds
  • 131 Cole Street, Masterton, near Douglas Park School
