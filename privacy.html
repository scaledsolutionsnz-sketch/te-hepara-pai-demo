Privacy

This website does not use contact forms, does not set tracking cookies and does not collect personal information.

When you tap a phone number your device makes a normal call. When you tap an email button, a draft opens in Gmail and nothing is sent until you choose to send it. The map is provided by Google Maps and the fonts by Google Fonts, which may receive standard technical information (such as your IP address) when those parts of the page load.

If you have any questions, please call Rev'd Robin Irwin, our Secretary, on 027 486 2714.
